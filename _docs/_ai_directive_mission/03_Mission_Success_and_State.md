# 03_Mission_Success_and_State.md

## What Success Looks Like

**12 to 18 months from October 2026:**

- Greg Paskal and Randy Rice have vetted a second ring of evaluators.
- Those evaluators are independently running interview evaluations, without the founders present.
- Guild Seals are being issued and posted on engineers' LinkedIn profiles, attesting to understanding and, where possible, execution at the earned level.
- Every issued Seal can be traced through its lineage to the founders.

**Longer term (observable signs the vision is working):**

- Employers recognize a Guild Seal and weigh it in hiring.
- The number of evaluators grows with the number of Seals, because of The Commission, without any money changing hands.
- A second chapter or a second trade adopts the framework without changing its principles.
