# 02_Mission_Audience_and_Scope.md

## The Audience

A credential is only worth something if two groups trust it: the person who earns it and the person who relies on it.

| Group | Who | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary (pilot)** | QA engineers in Rwanda | A credible, affordable way to prove real competence and a professional identity that does not depend on any one employer or institution |
| **Trusting party** | Rwandan employers, and international companies that hire or contract Rwandan QA engineers | A pre-vetted, trustworthy signal of actual skill, traceable to people they can recognize |
| **Long-term** | Practitioners of any trade, in any country | The same framework, applied to their own craft and Body of Knowledge |

---

## The Three Levels

| Level | Title | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Apprentice** | Learning the craft under the guidance of others. Trained at the Guild's regular gatherings. |
| 2 | **Journeyer** | Trusted to practice the craft independently. |
| 3 | **Steward** | Entrusted with the craft and the people in it. Evaluates Journeyers and vets new evaluators. |

Each level's evaluation is carried out by a member holding a higher level (Principle 5, Evaluate down).

---

## The Guild Seal and the Journey Book

**Guild Seal.** The single endorsement an evaluator gives when a candidate demonstrates competence at a level. A seal means a named person stands behind it.

> "Jean received her Apprentice Seal, sealed by Randy Rice."

The evaluator posts the Seal on the candidate's LinkedIn profile, attesting to the candidate's understanding and, where possible, execution at that level.

**Journey Book.** The engineer's public record. It holds every Seal they have earned and the lineage behind each one, back to the founders. It is what a hiring manager opens.

**What a Seal is.** A pre-vetting of a candidate's actual skill level by a known, traceable evaluator. It supplements an employer's own interview; it does not replace it. Its weight comes from the reputation of the people in its lineage.

## What This Is Not

These boundaries license any collaborator, including Claude, to push back when a proposal crosses them.

- **Not a for-profit organization, exam, or business** of any kind. This is the condition the project exists to overcome.
- **No candidate fees.** Earning a Seal is free to the candidate, always.
- **Not controlled by any single person, company, or institution**, including the founders.
- **Not a campaign against any other certification body.** The Guild defines itself by what it does, not by opposition.
- **Not a replacement for an employer's hiring process.** The Seal is a pre-vetting that informs it.
- **Not a test of memorized terminology.** Evaluation is of understanding and application.

---

## Safeguards

These exist so the Guild cannot be captured, as a professional community can be when one person holds the keys.

1. **Distributed authority.** At least two evaluators vouch for any new evaluator. No one person can create or block evaluators alone.
2. **Conflict-of-interest disclosure.** An evaluator declares any employment, business, or financial tie to a candidate, and steps aside where one exists.
3. **Revocable standing.** An evaluator who abuses the role can have their evaluating standing withdrawn by the members. Lineage shows every Seal that evaluator issued, so those can be re-reviewed.
4. **Member ratification.** Names, chapter leadership, and major changes are decided by members, not by the founders alone.
